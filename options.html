What data does this extension access?

Shortcut Hint Overlay operates 100% locally inside your browser. Here is an honest, specific breakdown of how permissions and data are handled:

  • Page HTML scan (on trigger only): When you hold the trigger key, the extension scans the active page's HTML for two standard shortcut attributes: accesskey and aria-keyshortcuts. It runs only when activated—never continuously or in the background.
  • No personal data or history collected: The extension does not collect, track, or transmit your browsing history, page contents, form inputs, passwords, or personal information.
  • No external server communication: Everything runs entirely on your device. The extension does not communicate with any developer server, analytics platform, or external API.
  • Settings stored locally: Your preferences (theme, trigger key, trigger mode, position, global shortcuts toggle, and disabled sites list) are saved in your browser via chrome.storage.sync, tied strictly to your Chrome profile.
  • Bundled shortcuts database: Curated site shortcuts are read from a static, bundled file (shortcuts-db.json) packaged with the extension itself, not fetched remotely.
  • Why all-sites permission is needed: Broad URL access is required solely so the overlay UI and shortcut detection can appear on any web page you visit—not to collect data from those pages.
